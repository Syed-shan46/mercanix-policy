Scope of Service

2. Scope of Tracking Services

Mercanix operates as a specialized Real-Time International Cargo & Consignment Tracking Platform designed for commercial importers, retailers, and clients managing orders outsourced through Hira Outsourcing:

  • Consignment Status Tracking: Real-time lookup of international air cargo and sea freight shipments from overseas origin warehouses (e.g. China Logistics Hub) to destination ports and domestic delivery addresses.
  • Milestone Monitoring: Verification of key shipping stages: Warehouse Receipt, Air/Ocean Transit, Port of Entry Arrival, Customs Clearance, Out for Delivery, and Final Delivered Confirmation.
  • Cargo Specifications: Review of itemized cargo metrics including gross weight (kg), volume in cubic meters (CBM), shipping mark codes, carrier names, and estimated delivery dates.
  • Business Profile Aggregation: Capability to view all concurrent consignments tied to an authorized Business Profile ID (e.g., HIRA-XXXXXX).

Operational Transit Variations: Estimated delivery dates and customs clearance schedules are subject to factors beyond our direct control, including international flight schedules, marine weather conditions, statutory customs documentation checks, port congestion, and local courier transit timelines.
