User Rights & Autonomy

How Data Erasure Works

At Mercanix (Hira App), we prioritize user autonomy and transparency. Depending on the type of data, erasure can be performed instantly on your device or requested from our central logistics records:

⚡ 1. On-Device Search History (Instant)

The app saves your last 8 queried tracking numbers locally in your device's sandboxed storage (SharedPreferences).

👉 How to erase: Tap the "Clear" button beside Recent Searches on the home screen, or uninstall the app.

🗑️ 2. Logistics & Profile Records

If your business desires to remove recipient phone numbers, client profile tags, or past completed shipment contact details from our CRM database:

Submit your request below or message our desk. Data is permanently purged from active systems within 7 business days.

Statutory Customs & Tax Retention: Finalized customs bills of entry, international shipping manifests, and tax invoices are retained for statutory audit durations (typically 5 to 7 years) as required under Indian Customs and GST statutory laws before permanent destruction.
