User Rights & Autonomy

How Account & Data Deletion Works

At Mercanix (Hira App), we respect your data ownership. When you request the deletion of your account, our engineering team initiates a structured purging procedure across our databases and connected services.

🗑️ Data That Is Permanently Deleted

  • Registered mobile phone number
  • Firebase Authentication account & security tokens
  • Merchant / business profile and contact names
  • Saved shipping and delivery addresses
  • Active shopping cart contents & custom RFQ draft inquiries
  • Verified supplier documents & badge credentials

📄 Data Retained for Legal Compliance

Under applicable statutory laws (including the Indian Goods & Services Tax regulations, Indian Companies Act, and financial dispute prevention), finalized transaction records, tax invoices, and payment IDs must be preserved for statutory tax audit periods (typically 5-7 years). These records are held in a restricted, archived ledger and are never used for marketing.
