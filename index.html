Section 1

1. Overview & Tracking Scope

Welcome to Mercanix (distributed as "Hira App", operated in association with Hira Outsourcing, hereinafter referred to as "we", "our", or "us").

Mercanix is designed as a dedicated real-time consignment and logistics tracking portal. It enables importers, retail merchants, and business partners to seamlessly look up their cargo consignments, international air/sea freight status, customs clearance checkpoints, container milestones, and domestic delivery updates using authorized Order IDs and Business Profile IDs.

Google Play Developer Policy Compliance: This Privacy Policy clearly details what information our application accesses, stores, and transfers when you track consignments, highlighting our strict privacy standards, zero intrusive permission profile, and transparent data practices.

This policy applies to the Mercanix mobile application on Google Play (Package IDs: com.mercanix.app and com.hira.hira_app), our backend tracking endpoints, and associated logistics communication desks.

Section 2

2. Information We Process

To deliver instant, accurate consignment tracking without requiring intrusive registrations, we process the following limited categories of information:

A. Search Queries & Tracking Tokens Provided by You

  • Consignment & Order Identifiers: When you search for a shipment, you enter an Order ID (e.g. HIRA-839201), Business Profile ID, or alphanumeric tracking token. This token is used solely to query live logistics records from our secure CRM tracking server.
  • On-Device Recent Searches: To save you from repeatedly re-typing long tracking numbers, your recent searches (up to 8 tokens) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android/iOS local storage (SharedPreferences). This search history remains on your device and is never uploaded, tracked, or sold to third parties. You can clear this history anytime with one tap.

B. Consignment & Logistics Information Displayed

Upon entering a valid token, the app retrieves and displays consignment information associated with that shipment:

  • Consignment Milestones: Real-time operational checkpoints including China Warehouse Receipt, Dispatch from Origin Port, Air/Sea Transit, Arrival at Destination Port / India, Customs Clearance Status, Out for Delivery, and Final Delivered status.
  • Cargo Specifications: Gross weight (KG), Cubic Meters (CBM), Shipping Mark, Shipping Method (Air Freight / Sea Cargo), and Warehouse Name.
  • Recipient & Multi-Item Summary: Business name, product item descriptions, declared quantities, delivery courier names, and expected delivery dates.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Network Connectivity State: Detection of internet availability to notify you when your device is offline and handle reconnection smoothly.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Metrics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic crash reports provided automatically via Google Play Services to resolve application glitches and improve stability.

Section 3

3. Real-Time Tracking Architecture & Milestones

Mercanix connects directly to the Hira Outsourcing CRM Tracking Engine (https://hiracrm.in/api/tracking/:token). The application processes status checkpoints through a structured 6-stage milestone tracker:

  1. Ready to Dispatch / China Warehouse: Consignment received, verified, weighed, and queued for international dispatch.
  2. Dispatched from China (In Transit): Cargo loaded onto scheduled air cargo or ocean shipping vessel.
  3. Arrived at Destination Port / India: Consignment touchdown at destination international airport or seaport.
  4. Under Customs Clearance: Assessment, documentation clearance, and duty verification by customs authorities.
  5. Out for Delivery: Handed over to domestic courier partners for last-mile transit to your business facility.
  6. Delivered: Successful final delivery confirmed with date and timestamp.

All communications between your mobile device and our logistics API occur over encrypted TLS/HTTPS protocols.

4. Device Permissions Explained

In adherence to Google Play Store minimum privilege guidelines, Mercanix requests zero invasive permissions. We only use the basic permissions required for internet connectivity and support communications:

🌐 Full Internet Access

android.permission.INTERNET: Required to send tracking queries to our API server at hiracrm.in and receive live shipment status updates.

📶 Network State Access

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects if your Wi-Fi or cellular network is active, preventing failed searches when offline.

💬 WhatsApp Support Launcher

Package queries: Allows you to tap "Chat with Logistics Desk" to launch your device's WhatsApp app with your Order ID pre-filled for immediate human assistance.

📞 Phone Call Launcher

Intent launcher: Allows you to tap "Call Logistics Helpline" to open your device's default dialer with our official helpline (+91 90720 14777).

Permissions We DO NOT Request: Our app does NOT request access to your GPS location, Contacts/Address Book, Camera, Microphone, SMS messages, Storage files, or Biometrics.
Section 5

5. Payments & Financial Safety

The Mercanix real-time tracking application is completely free to use.

Zero Collection of Financial Information: The tracking application does NOT collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. No in-app financial purchases or transactions occur within the tracking interface.

Any freight charges, customs duties, or consignment invoices related to Hira Outsourcing are invoiced and settled directly through authorized commercial banking channels with our operations office, independent of this tracking app.

Section 8

8. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We employ robust cybersecurity practices to ensure your tracking sessions are protected:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / 1.3): All API communications between the Mercanix app and the tracking server use industry-standard HTTPS encryption.
  • Sanitized Input Handling: Tracking tokens are sanitized and escaped against injection attempts before querying the database.
  • Isolated On-Device Storage: Recent search chips are kept strictly within private app sandboxed storage (SharedPreferences), inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
Section 9

9. Data Retention & Erasure Options

In full alignment with Google Play User Data policies, users have complete autonomy over their data:

Instant In-App Search Erasure

You can delete all stored recent search tokens directly from the app interface by tapping the "Clear" button next to the Recent Searches section. This immediately removes all saved tokens from device storage.

Consignment Record Removal Requests

If your business wishes to purge historical consignment contact details or client phone numbers from our logistics CRM database after delivery completion, you may submit a request through:

  • Our interactive Data & Account Deletion Portal.
  • An email to support@mercanix.com citing your Order ID or Business Profile ID.
  • Direct communication with our support desk at +91 90720 14777.

Verified erasure requests are processed within 7 business days, subject to statutory customs and tax record archiving requirements under applicable Indian laws.

11. Your Legal Rights (DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India, 2023) and international data privacy frameworks, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Erasure: Purging of personal contact identifiers from completed historical orders, as detailed in Section 9.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Dedicated support channel to resolve any privacy or data handling concerns.
