Section 2

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide our wholesale B2B marketplace features, manage logistics, authenticate merchant accounts, and process orders, we collect the following categories of data:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Phone Number & Authentication Credentials: When you register or sign in, we collect your mobile phone number. We utilize Firebase Phone Authentication to deliver a One-Time Password (OTP) via SMS for secure account access without requiring passwords.
  • Profile & Business Information: Store/business name, merchant contact name, email address, business category (e.g. Anti-Tarnish Jewelry, Electronics, Wholesale Sourcing), and business tax identifiers (such as GSTIN, trade licenses) submitted when applying for the Mercanix Verified Supplier badge.
  • Shipping & Delivery Addresses: Recipient name, full business address, pin code, state, city, and secondary delivery contact numbers used exclusively to fulfill wholesale dispatches.
  • Order & Cart Details: Selected wholesale products, product variants, order quantities, minimum order values (MOQ), custom quotation requests (RFQ), and communication with our support desks.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Hardware Data: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, unique device identifiers, network carrier, and system language settings.
  • App Performance & Log Data: Internet protocol (IP) address, application version, timestamp of interactions, error logs, and performance metrics collected to ensure application stability and security.

Section 3

3. Payment & Financial Data Handling

Mercanix operates a dedicated Escrow Trade Assurance system designed to protect wholesale buyers and suppliers. Under this model, buyer funds are held in secure escrow and released to suppliers only after delivery confirmation.

Zero Storage of Sensitive Banking Credentials: Mercanix does NOT collect, store, or have access to your Credit/Debit Card numbers, CVV codes, Net Banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are processed directly by certified payment gateways.

We integrate with Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited), an RBI-authorized and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment aggregator. When you pay inside the app:

  • Your payment credentials are encrypted directly between your device and Razorpay's banking servers.
  • Mercanix receives only non-sensitive transaction confirmation metadata: payment status (Success / Failed), timestamp, transaction amount, and a unique razorpay_payment_id used for generating tax invoices and processing escrow releases.
  • For orders placed via our dedicated WhatsApp Order Desk, payments are coordinated securely through official banking channels with verified invoices.
Section 4

4. Device Permissions Explained

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, we only request permissions strictly essential for core app capabilities:

🌐 Full Internet Access

android.permission.INTERNET: Enables communication with our product databases, Firebase authentication servers, and payment gateways.

📶 Network State

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects whether your device has an active internet connection to prevent data loss during order submission.

💬 SMS Retriever API

Google Play Services SMS Retriever: Allows automatic, instant autofill of the 6-digit OTP verification code without reading personal text messages.

🔗 External Application Launch

<queries> intent for WhatsApp: Enables opening WhatsApp directly with your pre-filled wholesale inquiry when using the WhatsApp Order Desk.

We do not access sensitive background location, contact address books, microphones, or SMS history.

Section 7

7. Data Security & Storage Practices

We employ industry-standard technical and operational safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the Mercanix app, our servers, and third-party APIs is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Restricted Access: Access to user contact details and order records is strictly restricted to authorized staff who require it for customer support or order fulfillment.
  • Tokenized Authentication: In-app sessions utilize secure cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and Firebase tokens that automatically refresh without persisting raw passwords.
Section 8

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In full accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, we empower all users with complete autonomy over their data.

How Long We Retain Data

  • Active Account Data: Your profile, delivery addresses, and cart items are retained as long as your account remains active.
  • Statutory Invoices & Tax Records: Under statutory accounting and tax regulations (such as the Indian Goods and Services Tax Act), transaction invoices, GST numbers, and payment receipts must be archived for a mandatory retention period (typically 5 to 7 years) after which they are permanently destroyed.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time without fee. You can initiate this in any of the following ways:

  1. Dedicated Deletion Portal: Visit our Account & Data Deletion Request Page and submit the simple verification form.
  2. Email Request: Send an email to support@mercanix.com from or citing your registered phone number with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Support Desk Hotline: Reach out to our business hotline at +91 97473 04599 (WhatsApp / Phone) to request assisted account removal.
Processing Timeline: Upon identity confirmation, your registered phone number, user credentials, active cart items, and saved addresses will be purged from our active databases within 7 business days.
